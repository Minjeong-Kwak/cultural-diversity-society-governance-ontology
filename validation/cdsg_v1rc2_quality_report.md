## Changes in rc.2

1. **Inconsistency of the rc.1 graph.** `cdsg:sourceInclusionText`, `cdsg:evidenceText` and `cdsg:sourceStatementText` had range `xsd:string` while their values carry language tags; HermiT reported rc.1 inconsistent. The ranges are now `rdfs:Literal`.
2. **Alignment review.** Two `invalid-legacy-link` alignments and the alignment from the Equality Act definition of race were moved to quarantine; three `needs-review` alignments were resolved as `related` (two) and `broader-than-curated` (one).
3. **Source status.** `ana:TargetCategorySourcingAssessment` records for each of the 242 curated categories, at 2026-08-27 and relative to the corpus: 9 membership specified (1 no longer in force, 8 in force), 6 related source category only, 2 context statement only, 225 no source statement identified.
4. **Worked case.** Executive Order 13166 (`00-20938.pdf`, p. 50121) defines persons with limited English proficiency; Executive Order 14224 sec. 3(b) (`2025-03694.pdf`, p. 11363) revokes it on 1 March 2025.
5. **Ontological status of categories.** `cdsg:PolicyTargetCategory` is a subclass of `gufo:NonSortal`; `cdsg:SourceSpecifiedPolicyTargetCategory` of `gufo:RoleMixin`, and is defined by equivalence as a category specified by some source statement; `ana:CuratedPolicyTargetCategory` of `gufo:Mixin`. Asserted category subtypes were removed from the data so that the reasoner classifies them.
6. **Design rationale.** `cdsg:designRationale` now states why policy content and evidence records are abstract individuals and on what the role types are founded.
7. **Language and provenance.** Eight Korean literals on non-Korean resources were removed under the project's language rule (source language plus English; Korean only for Korean sources). 23 `prov:wasDerivedFrom` links to unpublished pre-release identifiers were moved to quarantine.
8. **Scope.** 41 classes, 101 properties and 13 SKOS schemes that no rc.2 data or CQ exercises were separated into a draft policy-process extension outside the package, together with the observation module and its shapes. rc.2 keeps 42 CDSG classes, of which 41 have instances in the evidence set or by inference. The draft extension and rc.2 together are in the OWL 2 DL profile and consistent under HermiT.
9. **Document versions and effective periods.** `cdsg:NormativeDocumentVersion` (gufo:Kind, disjoint with `PolicyDocument`) records the text state cited from each of the 17 normative documents: 4 original texts (Regulation (EU) 2024/1351, Equality Act 2010 as enacted, Executive Orders as published in the Federal Register) and 13 official consolidations. Each version carries the publisher's identifier, `versionInForceFrom` (15 of 17) and `versionCurrentTo` (2 of 17) as the source states them, and one evidence record on the version header. All 20 evidence records on normative documents now name the version they cite and their source file. New effective periods: Executive Orders 14224 (from 1 March 2025) and 14398 (from 26 March 2026), and the general applicability of Regulation (EU) 2024/1351 from 1 July 2026 (Article 85). Five planning or reference periods whose evidence belonged to another document were removed; the reference period of *Les étrangers en France 2024* was relinked to its own evidence. The EU normative document was relabelled as Regulation (EU) 2024/1351, the instrument that contains the cited definition.
10. **Replacement of definitions.** `cdsg:DocumentSupersessionAssertion` records that a later document replaces an earlier one, with the date and the evidence of the source that states it. The corpus contains one such statement: the 2024 revision of SPD 15 (`2024-06469.pdf`, 89 FR 22182) replaces and supersedes the 1997 standards, effective 28 March 2024 for new requirements. The 1997 standards are represented as a cited document outside the collected corpus. The immigration-history concept of the Federal Statistical Office was checked and is a parallel definition, not a replacement; no relation is asserted.
11. **Evidence records.** Each of the 114 evidence records supports one claim and carries a locator and a verbatim excerpt with either file name and page or, for the eleven web records, address and retrieval time (rc.1 carried an excerpt on 30 of 60 and a locator on 25 of 60). Records that bundled several claims or pages were split into one record per claim; three records whose association with the Korean Ministry of Justice was marked ambiguous and had no page were replaced by the founding provision of the 4th Master Plan for Immigration Policy (p. 8); six records no claim used were removed. Every file excerpt was checked against the text of its PDF page with `pdftotext`. Korean locators on non-Korean sources were replaced by the source's own headings. `cdsg:evidencePage` is now functional, and the legacy `cdsg:evidenceAssociationStatusText` was removed.
12. **External identifiers and persistent IRIs.** `cdsg:publisherIdentifier` (a subproperty of `dcterms:identifier`) and `cdsg:publisherURI` (`xsd:anyURI`) record 46 identifiers and addresses on 22 documents and 4 versions: ELI `http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1351/oj` on the cited version of Regulation (EU) 2024/1351, legislation.gov.uk for the Equality Act and its as-enacted text, Justice Laws, Légifrance (document and dated version), e-Gov law IDs, Federal Register document numbers, ISBNs, ISSNs and catalogue numbers. Each value is printed in the source or was confirmed at the publisher. `NormativeDocument` and `NormativeDocumentVersion` point to `eli:LegalResource` and `eli:LegalExpression` with `rdfs:seeAlso`; no `owl:sameAs` is asserted. CP 1559 states that it replaces CP 1540, which is now a second document supersession assertion. The w3id.org redirect rules are in `publication/`; `publication/check_redirects.py` resolves all 961 CDSG IRIs to files in the package. The structural audit now reports references to undescribed individuals; it found six instants deleted with the misattributed periods, which were restored.
13. **Connectivity.** A check of the individual-to-individual links found the evidence set split into nine parts. Issuers were added to 15 documents from the enacting clause, issuing ministry, notice agency or publisher line printed in each source, with 11 new institutional actors (five legislatures including the European Parliament and the Council, one ministry and five statistical or budget offices) and the concept `legislature` in the actor-type scheme. `cdsg:administeredByInstitutionalActor` records the ministry printed in the header of the two Korean statutes, `cdsg:preparedByInstitutionalActor` replaces `dcterms:creator` for the integration report prepared by DeZIM, and the Federal Government Commissioner's post is linked to the Federal Government. `cdsg:withinJurisdiction` places France and Germany within the European Union. The Constitution Act, 1982, enacted as Schedule B to a UK Act, links Canada to the UK Parliament and gains its effective period from 17 April 1982. The evidence set now forms six parts (France, Germany and the EU; Canada and the UK; Korea; Japan; the United States; Australia). `cdsg:isNormativelyGroundedIn` and `cdsg:evidenceItemNumber` moved to the draft extension and the deprecated `cdsg:issuedBy` was removed; the seven properties without asserted values are inverses or super-properties whose values the reasoner derives. All 19 new excerpts were checked against their PDF pages.
14. **Grounds of disjointness.** All 20 classes in the 18 disjointness axioms carry a design rationale naming one of three grounds: different kinds, one function per statement record, or one specifying statement per source category. `SourceSpecifiedPolicyTargetCategory` now allows at most one specifying statement; the category-level disjointness then follows from the statement-level one (checked by removing the category-level axioms: the two-statement fixture stays inconsistent, and becomes consistent only when the cardinality axiom is also removed). The Canadian Anti-Racism Strategy and the Japanese Comprehensive Measures are linked to their files through issuer evidence; the English outline of the Japanese roadmap is linked to its file, and its issuer link, which no source supports, was removed. The Korean 4th Master Plan for Multicultural Family Policy (`제4차 다문화가족정책 기본계획_배포.pdf`), source of nine curated categories, was added to the data folder and linked through its founding provision (p. 15) and a 2023-2027 planning period.
15. **Issuers from web sources.** The five documents whose files name no issuer now have one: the National Diet for the two Japanese statutes (House of Representatives database of enacted laws, 120th and 198th Diet, recorded as their original texts as promulgated), the President of the Republic for CESEDA (Ordonnance n° 2020-1733 of 16 December 2020, recorded as the original text of the code's legislative part, NOR INTV2029043R), the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government for CP 1540, and the Immigration Services Agency as publisher of the English roadmap outline (published at its address). `cdsg:evidenceSourceURI` records the web address; the evidence-record shape accepts either a file and page or an address and retrieval time. Every policy document in the evidence set now has an actor.
16. **Metadata.** `dcterms:license` CC BY 4.0 on every module; no `owl:priorVersion`, because rc.1 was not published; `cdsg:retrievedOn` range `xsd:dateTime`; corrected ontology name in the core description.
17. **Release hygiene.** The 314 `cdsg:migrationNote` development notes on curated categories and alignments were removed and the property dropped from the core; the audit now fails if one returns. The vendored gUFO is graph-isomorphic to `gufo.ttl` at upstream commit f412254 and the vendored gUFO shapes are byte-identical to `gufoshapes.ttl` at commit b95e14b; both are pinned in `external_dependencies.lock.csv` with tool versions. The validation protocol, release gates, reproducibility protocol, claim discipline, author-input status and release checklist describe the gates as executed for rc.2. The rc.1 Pellet runner and local baseline script are replaced by stubs that point to the current scripts, and Owlready2 is dropped from the requirements. Development-stage wording (alpha versions, legacy taxonomy) was removed from three definitions, two design rationales and seven alignment notes, the unused `cdsg:replacesLegacyResource` was dropped, and `cdsg:evidenceText` is now functional, as every record carries one excerpt; the audit fails on alpha-version wording.
18. **Competency questions.** The eight single-purpose CQs of rc.2 were replaced by five composite questions, one for each requirement the source analysis yields: what kind of norm and which text a source category rests on (CQ1), the states of a missing or lapsed source (CQ2), alignments and the norm behind them (CQ3), versions, replacement and revocation (CQ4), and retrieval of curated categories by comparison facet (CQ5). Each CQ is now checked against expectations fixed from the source analysis before the query was run, separately from the regression digest. The CQ13 query of the earlier set joined the revoked document to its statements without the alignment to the curated category; CQ2 joins through the sourcing assessment.

## Known limits of this validation

- NT06 (an endurant typed by a policy target category is not thereby a `PolicyTargetRole` instance) holds because no axiom links category membership to the role; it documents a commitment. NT04 and the curated-and-source inconsistency fixture are the non-trivial separation tests.
- `PolicyTargetRole` has no instances in the evidence set; rc.2 represents no concrete bearers. Category membership is expressed by typing an endurant with the punned category (IT06); the provenance-bearing membership record moved to the draft extension.
- The force at the reference date of the eight in-force sources was checked on 20 October 2026, after the reference date: the cited version was still listed as current by the publisher (Canada, Korea, Germany), the cited CESEDA consolidation is dated 27 August 2026, the applicability of Regulation (EU) 2024/1351 began on 1 July 2026, and the Federal Statistical Office still published results by immigration history. A change made and reversed between the reference date and the check would not be detected.
- Version dates are taken from the version header. The Residence Act and Equality Act versions state no in-force date. For the two Korean acts, the Korean Law Information Center lists the cited version as the current one on 20 October 2026; for the other acts the existence of a later version was not checked beyond the currency checks above.
- `cdsg:retrievedOn` is recorded for 21 of 114 evidence records: the eleven web records and ten records whose file carries a browser print header (ABS, e-Gov, 1 September 2026) or a generation stamp (Légifrance, 31 August 2026). The other files state no retrieval time, and file-system dates were not used.
- Eleven evidence records come from web pages, not from files in the corpus, and carry an address and retrieval time instead of a file and page. Their excerpts were checked against the page text returned by a web fetch, not against a saved copy. The CESEDA ordonnance is cited from a reproduction of the Journal officiel text on gisti.org, because Légifrance could not be fetched; the CP 1540 issuer rests on a publication catalogue entry. Korea, Japan, the United States and Australia remain separate parts: no source in the corpus links them to another jurisdiction, and cross-national alignment of curated categories is an analytical judgement not made here.
- Only replacement stated in a source is recorded. Definitions amended in place, such as Multicultural Families Support Act art. 2 (amended 2011 and 2015), have only the cited version in the corpus, so their earlier wording is not represented.
- Document-level effective periods are recorded for six normative documents; for statutes the sources cited give version dates, not original commencement dates.
- The `w3id.org/cdsg` redirect is written and passes `w3id-check`, but it is not yet registered, and the hosting repository is not yet published; until both are done the namespace does not dereference.
- No publisher address is recorded for the two German statutes (gesetze-im-internet.de could not be checked) or the two Korean statutes (law.go.kr addresses are name-based); their citations and version identifiers are recorded.

## Interpretation

Passing these checks establishes properties of the formal artifact and the evidence set under the stated tests. It does not establish regulatory correctness, completeness or representativeness of the sources.
